How it works

One partner, any circular model.

Rental

  1. 01

    Choose the pieces

    Occasion wear, outerwear and statement pieces that deserve more outings.

  2. 02

    We present them

    Clear photos, fit notes and condition details for every piece.

  3. 03

    They circulate

    Customers rent them for events, travel and work, and every piece comes back to you.

Try before you buy

  1. 01

    Offer a trial period

    Customers take pieces home for a few days before deciding.

  2. 02

    Keep what they love

    They pay only for what they keep; the rest comes back to us.

  3. 03

    Nothing sits idle

    Returned pieces are checked and go back into rental or resale.

Resale

  1. 01

    Bring back pre-owned stock

    Returns, samples, past seasons or pieces bought back from customers.

  2. 02

    We assess and present

    Each piece is checked, graded and presented to your brand's standards.

  3. 03

    Sold again

    A second life for your pieces, with your brand still at the centre.

Take-back

  1. 01

    Invite customers back

    Customers return pieces they no longer wear, in store or by collection.

  2. 02

    We collect and sort

    Every piece is picked up, checked and sorted for its next life.

  3. 03

    Nothing goes to waste

    Pieces go on to resale, rental, repair or responsible recycling.

Your model

  1. 01

    Tell us how you work

    Your collections, your customers, and what you want circularity to do for your brand.

  2. 02

    We design it together

    Repair, upcycling, subscriptions or a mix of models, shaped around your brand.

  3. 03

    We run it with you

    From operations to customer care, so your team can stay focused on design.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is LOUP?

A Lisbon-first circular fashion partner for premium brands and boutiques. We set up and run the circular model that fits each brand: rental, try-before-you-buy, resale, take-back or a mix.

Which circular models do you offer?

Rental, try-before-you-buy, resale and take-back to start, plus repair, upcycling or a combination where it makes sense. We start from your brand, not from a fixed format.

Who can partner with LOUP?

Premium brands, independent designers and boutiques with well-made pieces. We're starting with a small group in Lisbon.

When does LOUP launch?

Soon, in Lisbon first. Join the waitlist and you'll be the first to know.

Can I join as a customer?

Yes. Depending on each brand's programme, you'll be able to rent, try before you buy, buy pre-owned or hand back pieces. Join the waitlist and we'll be in touch when LOUP opens.

What happens to my email?

We only use it to tell you about LOUP's launch and progress. Read our privacy policy.
